HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions.

Who can use this portal?

The portal is intended for Lipa City Colleges students, faculty, employees, and other authorized library users. Access requirements vary by platform: some use individual LCC identification, while others use institution-provided credentials.

Do I need an account?

Access depends on the resource. LIBROS-OPAC uses your Student or Employee identification, while some subscribed platforms use institutional access information provided by the library.

Can I use these resources outside the campus?

Remote access depends on the provider and its current settings. Review the access details shown in this portal before opening the resource.

Which resource should I use for research?

GALE is a good starting point for journal articles and academic research. IG Publishing and TRITECH offer additional digital books and references.

Where can I check whether the library has a book?

Use LIBROS-OPAC to search the Lipa City Colleges library catalog.

What should I do if a login does not work?

Contact the Lipa City Colleges Learning Resource Center and tell them which platform you are trying to access.

Can I share the institutional credentials?

Institutional access information is intended for authorized LCC users and should not be shared outside the institution.
